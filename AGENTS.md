# FCC Assistant — Agent Guidance

Before making or recommending any substantial product, architecture, data-model, AI-agent, permissions, document-workflow, or refinery-integration change, read and align with:

- `docs/MASTER_VISION.md`

Treat that document as the canonical product direction and guardrail set for this repository.

In particular:

- FCC is the proving ground, not the final product boundary.
- The target is a refinery-wide, role-aware, highly autonomous engineering and operations assistant.
- Preserve the read-only boundary toward process-control systems unless a separately approved safety architecture explicitly changes that scope.
- Engineering-document changes must follow controlled proposal/redline → review → approval → revision workflows.
- Prefer shared, scalable platform capabilities over one-off FCC-only implementations.
- Major implementation decisions should be reviewed against the checklist in `docs/MASTER_VISION.md` before being considered complete.
